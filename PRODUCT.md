# PRODUCT

## Register
brand

## Product purpose
Personal site for Gabriel "Gab" Lopez. Single page that an investor, recruiter, founder, or potential collaborator can land on, scan in 30 seconds, and walk away with a clear picture of who he is, what he's built, and how to reach him. The site IS the product — there is no app behind it.

## Users
- VCs and founders evaluating him as an operator (Backseat AI / Clout Kitchen context)
- Engineers considering joining a team he leads
- Journalists / podcasters looking for context before booking him
- Friends and acquaintances passing the URL around

## Brand voice
Three words: **wired, blunt, ship-it.** Confident operator, no marketing fluff, prefers facts over adjectives. The kind of person who would rather say "we hit 20k concurrent users" than "scaling rapidly." Light, dry humor is welcome; corporate enthusiasm is not.

## Anti-references (what this site is NOT)
- The standard dark-mode AI-startup portfolio (sky-indigo-purple gradient, glow blobs, glass cards, "Hi I'm <gradient name>")
- The big-agency designer portfolio (giant case-study reels, kinetic type, "selected works")
- The full editorial-magazine pose (drop caps, broadsheet grid, italic pull quotes)
- The crypto/neon-on-black operator look
- LinkedIn-style corporate restraint

## Strategic principles
- **Text first.** The content is the design. Imagery is incidental — there's no hero photo, no decorative illustration.
- **One page, long scroll.** No nav menus, no routes. Section labels along the side.
- **Press/awards earn their weight.** $4.5M from a16z, MedHyve acquisition, podcast features — these are real signals; surface them plainly without inflating them.
- **No template tells.** The first impression must NOT be "AI made that." The site should feel hand-written.

## Audience-specific cues
- **For investors:** keep numbers and outcomes specific (revenue, scale, funding). Date everything.
- **For engineers:** keep technical claims concrete (1k req/s, RAG, multi-agent LLMs).
- **For everyone:** the "Now" line and one piece of personality (e.g. dropped out of UP CS, plays games) signal a real human, not a LinkedIn profile.
